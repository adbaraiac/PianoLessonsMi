Frequently Asked Questions

What instruments do you teach?

We teach piano, violin, saxophone, cello, clarinet, guitar, drums, and baritone (and can discuss other instruments on request). We’ll match you with a teacher for your instrument and goals.

How much are lessons?

Weekly 30-minute lessons are $45 per lesson. We’ll confirm your teacher and schedule during signup.

Do you come to my home?

Yes! We travel to homes in Troy, Rochester, and nearby communities.

What ages do you teach?

We teach kids, teens, and adult beginners. We tailor the pace and repertoire to each student.

What if I don’t have an instrument yet?

For piano, a weighted keyboard is fine to start—we can help you choose one that fits your space and budget. For other instruments, we can point you toward rentals or starter options before your first lesson.
