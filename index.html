Quick Answers for Busy Parents

How does the free trial work?

One lesson at home. Meet your teacher, and we'll teach a simplified version of your child's favorite song. Decide when you're ready—no obligation, no card required.

Do you come to our home?

Yes—that's what we do across Troy, Rochester Hills, Birmingham, Royal Oak & Bloomfield Hills.

What ages do you teach?

Kids, teens & adult beginners. Pace and songs fit each student.

We don't have a piano or keyboard yet—now what?

No problem—we'll bring a keyboard to your first lesson so your child can try it out before you buy anything. If you enroll, we'll help you find the right one for your budget to keep at home.

What if my child doesn't click with their teacher?

Your free trial exists for exactly this. If it's not the right fit after enrolling, we'll match you with a different teacher at no extra cost—no awkward conversation needed.

How do you keep kids motivated to practice?

We start with songs they already love, then use a rewards program with stickers, weekly challenges, and certificates. Practice becomes something they ask for, not something you nag about.

How will I know if they're actually improving?

You'll get a short video update and progress note from the teacher every month, so you can see and hear exactly what they've learned.

What's your guarantee?

Try the trial lesson for free. If you enroll and, after your first full month, your child doesn't want to continue, we'll refund that month in full—no forms, no fine print.

How does billing work?

One simple monthly plan per student—no per-lesson invoices to remember or track down.

Why is enrollment limited?

Every lesson is private, in-home, and taught by one of our background-checked teachers—so we can only take on as many students as we can personally match and support well. That's also why spots are limited each season.
