PorchNote First Notes · beginners ages 5–9

Help your child play their first five piano songs at home.

Short, guided practices for beginners ages 5–9, with help when they get stuck. It all leads to a first concert for your family.

Try the first online lesson free

In the free lesson, your child learns the opening of Porch Light Steps and plays it on your own keyboard. No account, no card.

Have ready

  • A piano or keyboard. Every song uses just five white keys, from middle C up to G, so even a small 25-key keyboard works.
  • A tablet or laptop set beside it. A phone works too.
  • Sound on.

What you’ll do

  • Sit nearby. You don’t need to play piano.
  • Read the one-line tip if your child gets stuck.
  • Listen for the milestone each lesson names, and cheer.

From the first phrase to a family concert

One small step at a time, on your own piano or keyboard.

  1. 1Porch Light StepsThree notes, up and down. The free lesson starts here.
  2. 2Steady Drum MarchKeeping a steady beat.
  3. 3Five Little FriendsOne finger for every key, C to G.
  4. 4Hello, SunshinePlaying loud and soft.
  5. 5Mini Concert WaltzCounting in threes.
  6. 🎤Your family concertA guided rehearsal, then all five songs for the people they love.

When it gets tricky, help is one tap away

Every practice has an I’m Stuck button. Your child picks what helps, then goes right back to the same spot.

📍

See where to start

Hear it slower

Practice the tricky notes

Just the hard two or three notes on their own, then back into the whole part.

Self-paced: go at your child’s speed. The app can’t hear your piano, so playing on the real keys is marked done by your family — and you can help without knowing piano.
