Porchnote matches background-checked piano teachers with families in Troy, Rochester Hills, Birmingham, Royal Oak, Bloomfield Hills and beyond. We find the students, handle the scheduling and the billing, and leave you the part you actually trained for.

Apply to Teach

What You Get

  • Students, without the hustle. No studio rent, no flyers, no ads, no building a following. We bring you families who have already said yes.
  • You never chase a payment. We invoice the parents. You get paid for the lessons you teach, whether or not a family has paid us yet.
  • You set the map and the week. Pick the cities you’ll drive to, how far you’ll go, and which afternoons you actually want to work. We match around you.
  • No admin. Scheduling, cancellations, rescheduling and parent questions come to us, not to you at 9pm on a Sunday.

Who We’re Looking For

  • You can make a nervous seven-year-old feel brave in the first ten minutes.
  • You play well enough to teach beginners through intermediate comfortably.
  • You have your own transport—lessons happen in the family’s home.
  • You’re happy to complete a background check. Every one of our teachers has.
  • You can commit to the same weekly slot for a term, so families can build a routine.

Formal teaching experience helps, but it isn’t a dealbreaker. Several of our teachers started with us straight out of playing. What we can’t teach is whether kids like you.
