Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial lesson work?

Book a complimentary trial at your home. You’ll meet your teacher, see our teaching approach, and decide if membership is right—no obligation.

What’s included in monthly memberships?

Each plan includes weekly (or twice-weekly) private lessons, personalized curriculum, flexible scheduling, and progress support. See our membership options for details.

Do you come to my home?

Yes. We specialize in in-home piano lessons across Troy, Rochester Hills, Birmingham, Royal Oak, Bloomfield Hills, and surrounding Metro Detroit areas.

What ages do you teach?

We teach kids, teens, and adult beginners. Lessons are tailored to each student’s age, goals, and learning style.

What if we don’t have a piano yet?

A weighted keyboard works great to start. We’re happy to recommend options that fit your space and budget.

How are teachers matched?

We consider your child’s personality, experience level, and schedule to pair them with the best-fit background-checked instructor.
